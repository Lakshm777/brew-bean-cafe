Behind the Brew

Our Story

Born in the Peaks

A Trek, a Stove, and the Open Sky

Brew & Bean was conceived during a winter trek through the pine-laden ridges near Solang Valley, Manali.

Huddled in heavy wool blankets under a makeshift canvas shelter, we brewed a rustic pot of coffee over a tiny camping stove to escape a sudden snowfall. In that profound, snow-muffled silence, we realized that the best coffee isn't just about roasting profiles or extraction ratios—it's about the space and serenity surrounding the cup.

We set out to bring that quiet mountain clarity back with us. Brew & Bean is designed to replicate that very refuge: a serene space where the air smells of fresh pine needles, the view opens to majestic peaks, and every sip invites you to pause, breathe, and slow down.

Our Philosophy

Himalayan Stewardship

We believe in respecting the land that inspires us. Our cafe operates under a set of core principles designed to keep the Himalayas clean and support local mountain communities.

Ethical Micro-lots

We source our single-origin beans directly from organic shade-grown estates in Chikmagalur and the Nilgiri hills. By paying above-market premium prices, we ensure sustainable farming livelihoods.

Glacier Pure Water

The secret to our smooth espresso is local mountain water. We utilize an advanced eco-filtration system that purifies local stream water without wasting vital minerals or discharging chemical waste.

Mountain Support

A portion of every purchase goes directly into sponsoring waste cleanup drives along the trekking routes of Himachal Pradesh, keeping our valleys as clean as they were meant to be.
