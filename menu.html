Slow Sips & Fresh Ferments

Menu & Services

Every item on our menu is prepared with locally sourced mountain ingredients, mineral-rich stream water, and slow-fermenting artisanal methods. Clean, serene, and deeply nourishing.

Signature Brews

High Altitude Arabica ₹160
Single-origin light roast beans grown at 4,500ft in Chikmagalur. Brewed cleanly as a pour-over with notes of local pine-honey and orange zest.
Vanilla Bean Latte ₹240
A double shot of espresso paired with creamy organic milk, infused with genuine scraped vanilla beans and a touch of raw cane sugar.
Glacier Cold Brew ₹220
Slow-steeped for 18 hours in cold spring water, offering a velvety-smooth cup with natural sweetness and notes of roasted cocoa.
Ceremonial Matcha Latte ₹260
Authentic Japanese stone-ground matcha, whisked to a jade lather and poured over hot or iced plant-based milk of your choice.
Himalayan Forest Honey Tea ₹140
Infusion of local wild rosehips, spearmint, and chamomile, sweetened with hand-harvested wild mountain forest honey.

Alpine Eatery

Rustic Woodfire Croissant ₹180
Golden flaky pastry layered with organic grass-fed butter, baked to crispy perfection in our stone woodfire hearth.
Grilled Pesto Paneer Sourdough ₹280
Our signature 24-hour fermented house sourdough, grilled with cubes of soft organic paneer, house-made wild basil pesto, and fresh local greens.
River Pebble Chocolate Cookie ₹120
Soft-baked double dark chocolate chunk cookie, finished with coarse flakes of Himalayan pink rock salt.
Glacier Berry Chia Parfait ₹190
Organic Greek yogurt layered with high-altitude honey, chia seeds, and a fresh compote of wild mountain raspberries and blackberries.
Cafe & Roastery Services

More Than Just A Coffee Shop

In addition to our daily menu, we offer a selection of dedicated services designed to celebrate local mountain coffee culture and support quiet workspaces:

High-Altitude Coffee Tastings

Join our roaster every Saturday afternoon at 3:00 PM for an intimate guided journey through single-origin Indian coffees. Learn about altitude effects and clean pour-over techniques.

Quiet Workspace Desks

Need a quiet space to write, think, or code? Our upper floor features silent desks equipped with high-speed fiber internet, charging hubs, and panoramic windows facing the Beas river.

Whole Bean Retail & Merch

Take the mountain peace home with you. We sell fresh roasted beans in sealed eco-pouches, along with handcrafted ceramic mugs made by local Himachali potters.
