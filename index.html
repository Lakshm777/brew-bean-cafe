Welcome to Brew & Bean

A Quiet Sanctuary For Coffee Purists

Tucked along the banks of the Beas River in Manali, Brew & Bean brings the silence and purity of the mountains straight into your cup.

We roast our beans in small batches, selecting only single-origin high-altitude Arabica from organic farms. Paired with slow, artisan fermentation sourdoughs, every dish on our menu is designed to capture the slow, refreshing pace of mountain living.

The Brew & Bean Experience

Crafted by High Altitude and Mountain Soil

Altitude Sourced

Our Arabica beans are grown above 4,500 feet in shade-grown forest micro-lots, intensifying the notes of pine, honey, and wild berries.

Pristine Calm

Inspired by the stillness of deodar woods. We provide a workspace and relaxation haven free of city clutter and digital noise.

Artisan Woodfire

From paneer sourdough to woodfired croissants, everything is slow-fermented for 24 hours and baked fresh every morning.
